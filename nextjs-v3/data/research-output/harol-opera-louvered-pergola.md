## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Louvered pergolas may not be ideal for those looking for full protection from the elements, since they don't offer a complete seal against rain or heavy wind  _(källa: captainbobcat.com)_
- Rain Protection: When angled properly, louvers can provide a decent amount of rain protection. However, they are not fully waterproof.  _(källa: captainbobcat.com)_
- Water leakage is perhaps the most cited issue from pergola owners. Despite louvers closing fully, water can sometimes seep through or drip, particularly during heavy rain or storms. Causes include incorrect slope/installation, damaged seals or gaskets, and clogged overhead drainage systems.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_

## Vanliga problem

- Louver mechanism failure: louvers stop rotating or become difficult to adjust due to debris buildup in tracks, lack of lubrication, worn or damaged gears, misaligned louvers. Salt air near coastal areas accelerates wear.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Motor or remote control problems: motor won't respond, remote control fails, intermittent movement. Causes include dead batteries, signal interference, faulty motor or wiring, weather-related damage. Water intrusion can short electronics.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Corrosion or paint damage: UV exposure causes fading over time, abrasive particles wear the finish, salt air near coastal areas can cause minor surface corrosion or oxidation.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Structural instability or rattling noise: loose fasteners, shifting soil/foundation movement, improper installation, thermal expansion and contraction can cause wobbles, squeaks, or rattle in wind.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Uneven louvers or sagging from improper initial leveling, warp from thermal stress, or loose support brackets.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Insect or debris accumulation: bees, wasps, spiders, and leaves can accumulate in louvers, creating nests or blockages.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Gapless louver systems are recommended over older designs for areas with heavy rainfall or storms; standard louver systems may not provide sufficient weather protection in such conditions.  _(källa: azenco-outdoor.com)_
- If you live by the coast, salt air can quickly cause metals to rust if not treated. Specific material considerations are vital for coastal installations.  _(källa: azenco-outdoor.com)_

## Praktiska tips

- Apply a silicone-based spray lubricant to pivot points and tracks every 3–6 months. Avoid oil-based lubricants, which attract dirt.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Clean the louvered pergola at least twice a year using a mild detergent mixed with water and a soft cloth. Rinse all soapy spots with water.  _(källa: bonpergola.com)_
- Apply a clear automotive wax annually to protect paint from UV and moisture.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Perform a water test after installation. Check and tighten any loose screws or fasteners to prevent louver misalignment during rainfall.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Protect electronics from voltage spikes by installing a surge protector. Store the remote indoors when not in use.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Check built-in gutters, downspouts, and internal channels regularly. Remove leaves, twigs, or grit that block water flow.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- Regularly conduct an annual inspection of all fasteners, bolts, and screws. Loose connections can compromise the stability of the pergola, impacting its ability to withstand weather conditions.  _(källa: bonpergola.com)_

- Choosing wrong materials for your climate: selecting materials that aren't right for local weather conditions. Many homeowners focus on how a pergola looks more than how well it performs, leading to early damage and costly repairs.  _(källa: azenco-outdoor.com)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Check alignments of the frame and distance between all pillars before installing louvers. Measure diagonal distance between opposite corner pillars. If things are off, you'll have trouble adding louvers to the top.  _(källa: thediyplaybook.com)_
- Secure pillars to the ground by anchoring them with concrete footings. Dig holes at designated spots for each pillar and pour concrete into the holes. Insert the pillars into the wet concrete and allow it to dry.  _(källa: miradoroutdoor.com)_
- A pergola closer to the house will naturally be more stable than a free-standing one.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
- For soil installations, consider deeper footings or helical piers. Cross-bracing or diagonal supports can greatly increase rigidity. In windy environments, use wind straps or cable braces.  _(källa: serobanpergola.com)_
