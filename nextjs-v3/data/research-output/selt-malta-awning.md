> Malta is a light, free-hanging awning. Construction of the awning is characterised by universal brackets being simultaneously roller tube bracket, wall-ceiling bracket and arm bracket. Light and solid brackets forged from aluminium ensure durability and relatively low weight of the construction. Universal bracket provides two options of assembly, either to the wall or ceiling.

## Begränsningar

- At projection 310cm and width above 410cm (at any projection), increased deflection of the front beam occurs (zwiększone ugięcie belki przedniej)  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Projection is strictly dependent on the awning width – the wider the awning, the greater the possible projection. Narrowest widths (190–239 cm) only support 160 cm projection  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Widths from 471 to 500 cm only support 160 cm projection  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- No overlapping arms; no telescopic support (listed as 'no' in spec table for Malta, unlike larger models such as Jamaica or Silver Plus)  _(källa: pdf.archiexpo.com)_
- No optional hood available (listed as 'no' for Malta, while some other Selt models offer an optional hood)  _(källa: pdf.archiexpo.com)_
- No cassette – the roller tube mounting has no cassette enclosure; only optional half-cassette (półkaseta)  _(källa: pdf.archiexpo.com)_
- For PLAIN fabrics, the only available valance type is E  _(källa: pdf.archiexpo.com)_

## Vanliga problem

- When mounting with an additional protective half-cassette, standard Selt wall brackets must be used separately; in some situations it may be necessary to cut off part of the handle so that it does not interfere with the proper installation  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_

## Praktiska tips

- The awning is fully assembled and adjusted at delivery. Installation is basically very simple and is limited to attaching the brackets to the wall or ceiling.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Angle adjustment can be done entirely by yourself using an open-end wrench, the included nimbus, and a spirit level – tighten or loosen the awning nuts as needed  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- SELT recommends using Fischer fastening products selected according to the type of substrate. For uncracked concrete C20/25 class and no higher than C50/60, specific Fischer products are proposed.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- SELT recommends using only professional assembly crews, which guarantee correct installation  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- When ordering or pricing, always provide the total external width of the awning, including the handles. The reach is the degree of extension of the awning.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- The optional use of weather automation additionally protects the product against changing weather conditions  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Projection availability depends on width: 190–239cm → only 160cm; 240–470cm → 160 or 210cm; 290–410cm → 160, 210 or 260cm; 340–350cm → all projections up to 310cm; 471–500cm → only 160cm  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Vanliga felval

- In manual-drive version, the external dimension includes the crank joint of the awning – this must be accounted for in measurement  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Two mounting methods: wall and ceiling. The number of mounting brackets is always 2 extreme brackets.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Can be attached to a wall insulated with Styrofoam up to 20 cm thick  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- If mounting with additional half-cassette, use standard Selt wall brackets; the Malta awning has its own extreme brackets and the canopy is mounted on separate ones. May need to cut off part of the handle if it interferes with half-cassette installation.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- In case of installation in public places, e.g. hospitals, schools, boarding schools, the placement of switch/remote control device should be marked according to health and safety regulations  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
