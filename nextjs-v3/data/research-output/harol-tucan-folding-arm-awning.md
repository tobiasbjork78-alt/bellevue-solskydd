## Vanliga problem

- Cracks appearing in the facade to which the folding arm awning cassette is mounted and/or cassette coming loose — usually indicates facade is not resistant to the leverage caused by the awning. May need chemical anchors, extended roof supports or alternative sun protection product.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Cassette no longer closing completely, leaving a small part of the sunshade visible (and sometimes discoloured) — usually because almost all sunshades expand over time. Usually easily remedied by reprogramming the motor setting.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Front panel hangs higher on one side than the other — can usually be adjusted using adjusting screws; the affected arm must be held up during adjustment to avoid damage.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Sun sail no longer neatly tight — usually because fabric expands over time. Fix by reprogramming motor adjustment.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- No response from remote control — usually means battery needs replacement; reprogramming may be necessary.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Sagging fabric from strong winds whipping the awning back and forth, possibly overextending the canopy.  _(källa: rollac.com)_
- Motor issues: awning jams or jerks when retracting — most complex mechanical issue, best handled by a professional.  _(källa: rollac.com)_
- Fabric damage from tears, UV fading, mold and mildew — repair with special patches/tape; waterproof adhesive for waterproof fabric. Mold prevention: spray with hose, apply mildew remover, let dry completely before retracting.  _(källa: rollac.com)_
