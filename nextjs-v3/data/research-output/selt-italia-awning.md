> Italia is a window and balcony awning for shading and protection of window niches, shop windows, balconies, terraces and verandas. Aluminium drop arms are equipped with a spring which tensions the fabric and prevents the arms from folding under wind pressure. The awning's weight is distributed between the arms and roller tube brackets. Thus ITALIA can be installed even in places where the strength of mounting surface excludes use of a terrace awning with folding arms. The light construction makes awning installation very easy. Construction elements are powder lacquered in white colour.

## Vanliga problem

- Due to fabric being double folded at seams, diameter of roller tube varies in different places, resulting in 'embossing' or 'waving' effect near the seams, in the middle of the fabric or along the edges  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Creases may appear while the textile is rolled up; in the area of creases a delicate line could be visible in certain light — mainly applies to dark-colored textiles  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Side strips are particularly exposed to heavy load, therefore a slight drop of the edges may occur  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Acrylic material coated with fluororesine makes it stiffer, which may result in the formation of micro-clearances near folds, wrinkles or damage to the fabric  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Due to use of impregnating agent, fabric is sensitive to all kinds of scratches that are difficult to avoid even with very precise and careful production process — applies primarily to monochromatic materials  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Factors such as acid rain, animal-origin contamination and cleaning with abrasive agents can destroy the protective coating of the fabric  _(källa: ceneo.pl)_
