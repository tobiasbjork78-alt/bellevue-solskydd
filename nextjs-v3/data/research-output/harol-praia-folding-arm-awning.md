## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Wind class 2 (EN13561) — rated for wind speed 7.5–10.4 m/s only. Must be retracted in stronger winds.  _(källa: zenronline.eu)_
- Open box system (cover is optional, not standard) — without cover, fabric and mechanism are more exposed to weather when retracted compared to fully enclosed cassette models.  _(källa: harol.com)_
- Max single-unit width is 7 m; wider installations require coupling.  _(källa: harol.com)_

## Vanliga problem

- Folding arm awning cassettes can develop cracks in facade mounting points or come loose due to leverage forces, especially if facade is not resistant enough. May require chemical anchors or extended roof supports.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Almost all sunshade fabrics expand over time, causing: cassette not closing completely (leaving part of fabric visible and sometimes discoloured), and fabric no longer being neatly taut. Usually remedied by reprogramming motor settings.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Front panel hanging higher on one side than the other — usually adjustable with adjusting screws but requires holding up the affected arm during adjustment.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Wireless remote control stops working — usually a battery replacement issue, reprogramming may be necessary.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Fabric tears from wind-blown debris, tree branches, or UV degradation over time — common to all retractable awnings, particularly in high-UV environments.  _(källa: cflawnings.com)_
- Retractable awning sagging or loose areas over time due to stretched fabric or improper tension adjustments.  _(källa: aladdininc.net)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Never leave folding arm awning extended when you are not around — sensitive to wind gusts which can result in considerable damage.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- If facade material is not resistant to the leverage caused by the folding arm awning, alternative mounting or alternative products (e.g., freestanding pergola awning) may be needed.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Praktiska tips

- Use your folding arm awning regularly and watch for abnormalities, acting on them immediately. After a period of non-use, always pay extra attention.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Wind sensor and automatic retraction strongly recommended; one supplier insisted on them or would not guarantee against wind damage. Automatic retraction credited with saving awnings on multiple occasions over 10 years.  _(källa: choice.community)_
- Never force a stuck awning to extend or retract — this often causes tears at stress points. Instead, investigate the underlying mechanical issue.  _(källa: cflawnings.com)_
- For front panel not being level: adjustable with adjusting screws, but the affected arm must be held up during adjustment; otherwise nothing happens and/or damage risk.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Fabric stretching causing cassette not to close completely is normal over time; fix by reprogramming motor end-stop settings.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Always contact original installer first before DIY repairs — warranty may still apply, and self-repair can invalidate existing warranty.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Vanliga felval

- Mounting to a facade that cannot handle the leverage forces of a folding arm awning — leads to cracking and loosening. May need chemical anchors, extended roof supports, or entirely different product.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Choosing not to include wind sensors — forum contributor notes this led to a near-catastrophic wind event where awning nearly ripped bricks from the wall.  _(källa: choice.community)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Praia can be secured directly to the wall. Harol offers three mounting options across range: recessed, ceiling, or against the wall.  _(källa: zenronline.eu)_
- Folding arm awnings are 'not complex to install, any competent handyman could do it as long as they had help to lift the big awnings. Of course if they are electrically connected you should get an electrician to do that work.'  _(källa: choice.community)_
- Harol invested in articulated arms that can be easily assembled — ease of assembly highlighted as design priority after almost two years of R&D.  _(källa: zenronline.eu)_
