> Casablanca is a cassette awning perfect for large terraces. ## Praktiska tips

- The product is intended for self-assembly. Assembly is basically very simple and is limited to mounting the brackets to the wall/ceiling/rafter.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- When using a chemical anchor with rods, you can mount the awning on polystyrene insulation without cutting holes. This set is especially recommended for awnings with a long projection, e.g. 250cm.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- To change the tilt angle: fully extend the awning, loosen the internal screw and M10 Allen screws securing the movable part of the wall brackets, then turn the hex-head adjustment screw (unscrew to decrease angle, screw in to increase). Use a spirit level to check the front beam is level.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Brackets can be moved along the cassette within a certain range. Details are in the manufacturer's installation instructions and technical declaration.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Wall brackets are painted in the same colour as the structure. The number of pieces is adjusted to the width of the awning (2 brackets for widths 200–410cm, 3 brackets for widths 411–600cm).  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Ceiling bracket is joined (screwed) together with the wall bracket to form one unit; same for rafter bracket. The number of ceiling/rafter brackets always matches the number of wall brackets.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- If needed, it is possible to use more brackets than the standard number provided by the manufacturer.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
