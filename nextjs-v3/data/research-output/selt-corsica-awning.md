> Corsica is an awning designed with the idea of shading modern terraces in mind. Its construction is made from extruded aluminium, which ensures strength and durability of the awning, and Flyer's chain used in the arms in connection with precisely made joints guarantees its long-term dependable operation. The cassette is supported by square bar providing stability for the whole construction while making the installation easier. Corsica was designed to provide the possibility of installing LED lighting which allows the user to fully enjoy pleasant summer evenings. This awning, thanks to its aesthetically pleasing design can, apart from shading from the sun, serve as a perfect decorative element of the building, highlighting its individual and unique character.

## Praktiska tips

- The awning is delivered fully assembled and adjusted — installation is limited to mounting the brackets to the wall (or ceiling/rafters), placing the awning on them, and securing with screws  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Wall brackets are standard and painted to match the structure colour. Ceiling (2) and rafter (3) brackets are extras that join (twist) together with the wall bracket (1) to form one unit  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- When using a chemical anchor with rods, the awning can be mounted on polystyrene insulation without cutting holes — especially recommended for awnings with large projection e.g. 250 cm  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- The tilt angle can be freely adjusted as needed  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- The product is intended for self-assembly  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- The standard number of mounting brackets (from 2 to 5) depends on the width of the awning; more brackets can be ordered additionally if needed  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Monteringskunskap
