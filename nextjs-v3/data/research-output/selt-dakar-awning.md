## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Gears 11:1 are not equipped with overturn protection (so called mechanical end stop). During operation of awning with manual drive the force working on the crank is multiplied by gear mechanism and putting too much force can lead to fabric damage, especially when closing the awning.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Do not allow the fabric to be rolled in the opposite direction (from the bottom of roller tube), since it may cause its ripping off the roller tube, fabric damage or incorrect awning functioning.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Optional hood: No — no separate optional hood available for the Dakar model.  _(källa: aluprof.com)_
- Internal application: No — the awning is for external use only.  _(källa: aluprof.com)_
- Only the standard version of telescopic supports is available for the Dakar awning (no advanced variants).  _(källa: aluprof.com)_
- After fabric gets wet, it must be dried well to prevent damage. Using an impregnating agent at the start of the season is recommended to extend fabric life.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Non-standard valance height (beyond 21 cm) will affect price, and maximum 40 cm is not always possible to produce.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Vanliga problem

- Self-regulation of extreme position by an untrained person may lead to product damage.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_

## Praktiska tips

- The awning is delivered fully assembled and adjusted. Installation is limited to attaching the brackets to the wall (or ceiling/rafters). The tilt angle can be freely set after mounting if needed.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Wall brackets should be mounted in the place marked by the manufacturer at the back of the cassette, so that they are in the symmetry axis of the arm brackets.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Number of brackets depends on width: S ≤ 4.1 m requires 2 handles; S > 4.1 m requires 3 handles. This quantity is obligatory and the awning is standardly equipped by the manufacturer with this number.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Console brackets have 4 mounting points each. The number of brackets ranges from 2 to 4 depending on the width of the awning. Available in wall, ceiling, or rafter versions.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Telescopic supports work well especially with manual drive as additional support for the front bar.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- When ordering, always provide the total external width of the awning including the handles. Projection refers to the degree of extension.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Prior to first start of the electrically driven product, electrical measurements should be performed by a person holding adequate permissions. Do not start the motor without checking correctness of system installation.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- Using an impregnating agent at the beginning of the season is a good practice to extend fabric lifespan and utility.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Vanliga felval

- Manual crank operation without overturn protection means applying too much force when closing can damage the fabric. Users should turn the crank only until gentle resistance is felt.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Awning opening with manual drive is performed by making enough turns with the crank until the arms are opened, keeping the fabric tension. Closure by turning the crank until gentle resistance is felt.  _(källa: manualslib.com)_
- The awning can be installed on insulated walls (e.g. polystyrene up to 20 cm) without cutting out the insulation, using a special chemical anchoring kit.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Three mounting methods: wall brackets (standard, most common), ceiling brackets (extra fee), roof rafter brackets (extra fee).  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
