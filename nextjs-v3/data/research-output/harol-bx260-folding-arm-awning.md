## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Drop limited to max 3.5 m (compared to Harol's CADIZ, LUX, and PRAIA models which go up to 4 m)  _(källa: harol.com)_
- Folding arm awnings are sensitive to wind (gusts) — 'never leave your folding arm awning in the off/on position when you are not around, as it is sensitive to wind (gusts) and this can result in considerable damage'  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Vanliga problem

- Cracks appear in the facade to which the folding arm awning cassette is mounted and/or it comes loose — usually an indication that the facade is not resistant to the leverage caused by the awning. May need chemical anchors, extended roof supports, or an alternative product.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Cassette no longer closing completely, leaving a small part of the sunshade visible (and sometimes discoloured) — caused by fabric expanding over time. Usually remedied by reprogramming the motor setting.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Front panel hangs higher on one side than the other — can usually be adjusted using the adjusting screws; the affected jib arm must be held up during adjustment otherwise nothing will happen and/or you risk damage.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Sun sail no longer neatly tight — almost all sun sails expand over time; usually fixable by reprogramming the motor adjustment.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Remote control stops working — usually means the battery needs replacing; reprogramming may be necessary.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Discoloration occurs when a part of the sunshade remains in the sun and/or is exposed to weather conditions more than the rest.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Facades not resistant to the leverage caused by a folding arm awning — may require chemical anchors or alternative product like a freestanding pergola awning  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Praktiska tips

- Assess installation area for obstructions such as protruding pipes or electrical wires that may interfere with the awning's operation. Identify suitable mounting points that can support the weight and withstand external forces such as wind.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- Choose the appropriate bracket type based on the mounting surface and ensure secure attachment to prevent movement or instability. Properly positioning the brackets is crucial for overall stability and functionality.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- After installation, extend and retract the awning several times to check for smooth operation and proper alignment. If any issues arise during testing, make the necessary adjustments promptly.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- For motorised awnings, take precautions to ensure proper wiring and electrical connections to prevent the risk of electrical hazards.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- Regular maintenance: keep awning fabric clean by removing debris and dirt, lubricate moving parts to prevent rust and corrosion, and conduct seasonal inspections for signs of wear and tear.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- Use your folding arm awning regularly and watch out for abnormalities, acting on them immediately. After a period of non-use, always pay extra attention.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Never leave your folding arm awning extended when you are not around, as it is sensitive to wind gusts and this can result in considerable damage.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Always contact the original installer first when problems arise, as you may still be able to make use of the warranty — or even invalidate existing warranty by doing it yourself.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Vanliga felval

- Mounting on a facade that cannot resist the leverage forces of a folding arm awning — can lead to cracks and the cassette coming loose  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Align the folding arms according to the manufacturer's instructions and ensure they are properly secured to the brackets — critical for smooth operation and preventing unnecessary strain on the awning mechanism.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- When adjusting an uneven front panel, the affected jib arm must be held up during adjustment — otherwise nothing will happen and/or you risk damage.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Awnings may be too long for standard lifts in apartment buildings and may need to be hoisted externally (one customer's awning had to be hoisted three levels up with ropes, requiring five people).  _(källa: lifestyleawnings.com.au)_
- Safety should be a top priority when installing, especially when working at heights — use appropriate safety gear such as harnesses and helmets and secure the work area.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
