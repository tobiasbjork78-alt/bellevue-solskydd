> Harol's Coolscreen 7, the ideal surface-mounted screen or external roller blind for narrow window profiles and corner windows. Efficient sun protection, durable materials and stylish design increase comfort and energy-efficiency, allowing you to opt for long-term enjoyment. Can be fitted in front of the window, in the window opening or on the façade. With cable guides, without a zip system, these external blinds are ideal for corner windows and very narrow window profiles. Coolscreen 7 is characterised by its refined design, with a choice between a minimalist rounded or rectangular box. The look is completed by a lack of visible screws on the outside. With shade creation of up to 9 m² and a guarantee of 5 years, Coolscreen 7 combines reliable durability with excellent performance. Moreover, it was tested up to 15,000 cycles. This equates to being opened and closed once a day for 27 years.

## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Coolscreen 7 uses cable guides without a zip system, meaning it is not a zip screen — this makes it ideal for corner windows but implies less wind resistance and fabric stability compared to zip variants  _(källa: harol.com)_
- Standard (non-zip) screen blinds: fabric may flap in the wind; lower/medium wind resistance; limited insect protection; smaller maximum dimensions compared to zip screens  _(källa: nerligruppen.com)_
- Maximum surface area for Coolscreen 7 is 9 m² (max width 3.5 m, max height 3.5 m), which may be insufficient for larger façade openings  _(källa: harol.com)_
- Screen blinds in general do not completely darken the interior  _(källa: nerligruppen.com)_

## Vanliga problem

- Generic zip screen/track systems: 'They all start to breakdown after the 4th or 5th year' (general comment about zip track blinds, not Harol-specific)  _(källa: reddit.com)_
- Generic zip track blinds: 'Zip tracks arent designed to be kept closed in high speed winds' — they can act as 'giant sails'  _(källa: reddit.com)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Standard screen blinds (non-zip) are not suitable for windy areas or intensively used spaces — ZIP screen is recommended instead  _(källa: nerligruppen.com)_

## Praktiska tips

- When used externally, darker colours are more effective at reducing heat-gain and give better view-through  _(källa: solar-shield.co.uk)_
- If designing a new build, design in recessed pockets to accommodate any shading product so that they disappear from view when not in use. Get power installed into these pockets.  _(källa: solar-shield.co.uk)_
- Check the orientation of your extension, plan for shading early and speak to an expert about the options available  _(källa: solar-shield.co.uk)_
- For curtain walls with narrow window profiles: the SC800-ZIP Facade's 'Protect Glass' mounting profile is simply fixed using one screw, after which guide rails attach with a clip — avoiding risk of glass breakage  _(källa: archello.com)_

## Vanliga felval

- Choosing a standard screen blind instead of a ZIP screen for windy locations or large glass surfaces — standard screens may flap in the wind and have lower wind resistance  _(källa: nerligruppen.com)_
- If using separate vendors for blind manufacturing and installation: 'If something goes wrong with them, both vendors are likely to point the finger at the other and nobody's going to take responsibility to fix it'  _(källa: reddit.com)_
