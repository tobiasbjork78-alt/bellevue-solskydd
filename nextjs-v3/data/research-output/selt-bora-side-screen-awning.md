## Praktiska tips

- The wall mounting bracket is placed at height H1, calculated as: H1 = H/2 + 80mm. E.g. When H=1600mm, H1 is 880mm.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- The side awning is closed by releasing the handle lock and rolling it up manually until the front beam is placed in the awning cassette.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Check for obstructions such as gutters, pipes, or electrical wiring that may interfere with installation. Use a stud finder to identify secure mounting points.  _(källa: theoutdoorlivinggroup.com)_
- Apply weatherproof sealant at connection points to protect against moisture infiltration.  _(källa: theoutdoorlivinggroup.com)_
- Opt for stainless steel hardware to prevent rust; test the awning by retracting and extending it multiple times after installation.  _(källa: theoutdoorlivinggroup.com)_

## Vanliga felval

- Inaccurate measurements – double-check dimensions to prevent misalignment. Measurements should be taken at multiple points to account for surface-level variations.  _(källa: theoutdoorlivinggroup.com)_
- Ignoring surface stability – weak or uneven surfaces may require reinforcement before mounting.  _(källa: theoutdoorlivinggroup.com)_
- Skipping a test fit – temporarily positioning components before drilling ensures a precise fit.  _(källa: theoutdoorlivinggroup.com)_
