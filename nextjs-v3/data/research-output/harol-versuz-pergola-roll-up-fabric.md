## Praktiska tips

- Generic motorized roller shade maintenance: clean monthly by wiping fabric and roller tube with dry brush to remove debris; protect from rain by adding waterproof cover or retracting during heavy rain to prevent motor water damage; check power seasonally before summer and winter  _(källa: hotelworksgroup.com)_
- If roller shade won't roll up: debris buildup between fabric and roller tube is most common cause (fixes 60% of outdoor shade issues). Use soft-bristle brush, dry microfiber cloth, compressed air for motor vents. Use silicone-based lubricant (avoid oil-based as they attract dirt and can damage fabrics)  _(källa: hotelworksgroup.com)_
- For misaligned brackets: replace rusted screws with stainless steel screws for outdoor rust resistance; use a level to ensure roller tube sits straight (a crooked tube can't roll up evenly)  _(källa: hotelworksgroup.com)_
