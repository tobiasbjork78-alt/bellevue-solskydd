## Tillverkar-beskrivning (RÅMATERIAL — EJ KOPIERA)

**Kanal-typ:** tredjepart-fallback
**Källa:** `pdf.archiexpo.com` — https://pdf.archiexpo.com/pdf/selt-sun-protection-systems/awnings/65037-213265.html
**Varning:** RÅMATERIAL FÖR FÖRSTÅELSE — FÅR EJ KOPIERAS ELLER PARAFRASERAS NÄRA. Writer-voice skriver originaltext i Bellevues röst; denna text är endast brief.

> Australia awning is a non-cassette system in which the structure is based on a supporting square bar. The construction of the awning allows for installation even on small mounting surface. With big awning widths an additional reinforcement of the roller tube is used to prevent its deformation and uneven rolling of the fabric. It is also possible to use telescopic supports in this system. The arms are equipped with Flyer's chains which guarantee their reliable and safe operation even with large awning dimensions. The construction of the awning is made of extruded aluminium which guarantees its high durability and longevity. Brackets made from pressure cast and extruded aluminium provide high durability with relatively low weight. Square construction beam provides relative freedom of bracket placement.

## Begränsningar

- No cassette protection – it is a non-cassette system (no optional hood either)  _(källa: aluprof.com)_
- Overlapping arms not available with a projection of 360 cm  _(källa: aluprof.com)_
- Projection is strictly dependent on width – narrower awnings have limited projection options: width 195–244 cm allows only 160 cm projection; width 245–294 cm allows 160 or 210 cm; width 295–344 cm allows up to 260 cm; width 345–394 cm allows up to 310 cm; only width 395–700 cm allows the full 360 cm projection  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- In some cases the maximum tilt angle is limited to 40 degrees (rather than the stated 60 degrees)  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- External application only – not suitable for internal use  _(källa: aluprof.com)_
- Width dimension production tolerance of ±2 cm  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Praktiska tips

- Installation is simple and limited to attaching the brackets to the wall or ceiling. The awning arrives fully assembled and adjusted. The tilt angle can be freely adjusted after installation.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- The number of mounting brackets (from 2 to 5) depends on the width of the awning. The manufacturer sets a standard number of brackets for given dimensions; if necessary, it is possible to use more brackets.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Three arms are mounted at larger widths for stability: at projection 160/210/260 cm – 3 arms from width 531 cm; at projection 310 cm – 3 arms from width 591 cm; at projection 360 cm – 3 arms from width 651 cm.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- With big awning widths an additional reinforcement of the roller tube is used to prevent its deformation and uneven rolling of the fabric.  _(källa: aluprof.com)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Tilt angle adjustment procedure: Unroll awning completely, find arm holder with adjustment screw, loosen M70 screw with Allen key, use open-end wrench to unscrew (increase angle) or tighten (decrease angle) the adjusting screws, tighten M70 screw, use spirit level to check front beam is horizontal.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Can be mounted to walls insulated with Styrofoam up to 20 cm thick without problems.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Three mounting methods: wall mounts, ceiling mounts (combined with wall mounts), and rafter mounts (integrated with wall mounts).  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
