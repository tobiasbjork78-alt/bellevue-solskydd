> Casablanca is a cassette awning perfect for large terraces. Thanks to its built this awning is not only a perfect way of protecting from the sun, but also an aesthetic decorative part of the building. Specially designed construction from extruded aluminium ensures strength and durability of the awning, and Flyer's chain used in the arms guarantees its long-term dependable operation. Wide awnings are equipped with roller tube support to ensure that the fabric is evenly tightened. Special gaskets placed in the cassette further protect the fabric from weather conditions. What is more, properly selected electric control with weather sensors will make handling the Casablanca awning very easy and functional.
