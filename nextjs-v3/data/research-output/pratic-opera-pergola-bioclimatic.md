## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Vision model has lower max height (270 cm) and smaller post profiles (10 × 15 cm) vs Opera (300 cm, 15 × 15 cm); Opera needed for Raso and Connect compatibility  _(källa: architonic.com)_
- 6 post profiles are required if number of blades exceeds 29, potentially impacting open-plan layouts for larger configurations  _(källa: architonic.com)_
- Adding heating at a later date can be costly, not to mention, disruptive to the overall design of your garden and pergola — heating must be planned from the beginning  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Watch out for bioclimatic pergolas with lower-grade aluminium, thinner leg supports, and narrow roof blades, as well as the quality of the powder coating, which is often liable to flake (general category warning from dealer, not Opera-specific limitation)  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_

## Återförsäljarkunskap


## Praktiska tips

- The floor, supported by an iron substructure, frames Opera's self-supporting profiles, allowing for a firm hooking — a raised floor/platform approach was used in the Lonato del Garda installation to house the pergola  _(källa: designdiffusion.com)_
- Most integrated heater systems will require an electrical supply installed during the building process and must be included in the drawings before the structure has been approved and finalised  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_
- All Pratic pergolas are delivered with the pre-mounted electrical system and this will be reflected in the design right at the beginning of the project for electrical connection  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_
- Make sure that the fabrics available offer a sun filter for sun protection or that they have blackout fabric in different colours to suit your overall design  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_
- One remote should manage the entire setup (besides the heating, which is separate), giving you functions and variants to operate the blades, Raso drop awnings, and customisation of your chosen lighting system  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_
- For general louvered pergola installations in coastal/windy areas: making sure the mounting system was overbuilt for the wind ratings in their area was the key factor; the maintenance difference between aluminum and wood in those conditions is night and day  _(källa: reddit.com)_

## Vanliga felval

- Not planning heating integration from the start: 'Adding heating at a later date can be costly, not to mention, disruptive to the overall design of your garden and pergola'  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_
- Choosing cheaper louvered systems with lower-grade aluminium, thinner leg supports, narrow roof blades and poor powder coating quality  _(källa: skyeoutdoorliving.co.uk)_
