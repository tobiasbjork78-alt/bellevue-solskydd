## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Maximum single-module dimensions: 450L × 610P cm, H max 300 cm — larger installations require multiple modules  _(källa: archiproducts.com)_
- Brera P post profiles are 15×15 cm (larger than Brera S at 10×15 cm), which affects visual footprint  _(källa: archiproducts.com)_

## Praktiska tips

- For warranty/assistance claims, customer must register on the dedicated portal (customercare.pratic.it) and provide the product serial number found in the 'Uso e Garanzia' document or on a label on the product  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_

## Vanliga felval

- Brera S vs Brera P confusion: Brera P is the full-optional version compatible with Raso awnings and Connect glass panels; Brera S is not compatible with these accessories. Choosing Brera S when perimeter closure is needed would be a mismatch.  _(källa: archiproducts.com)_

## Monteringskunskap

- Installation quality matters — positive reviews specifically mention installer attention to detail as critical: 'attenti ai dettagli (che e' la parte sempre piu delicata di un lavoro artigianale)'  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_
