## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Never use the awning during heavy rain, storms or frosty conditions. Do not extend the awning when wind is above Beaufort Scale 5 (30-38 km/h). Otherwise, the awning will be damaged and can cause risk for users.  _(källa: lifestyleawnings.com.au)_
- A slope of minimum 15 degrees should be maintained on your awning to stop fabric sagging and provide water run-off if to be used in rain.  _(källa: lifestyleawnings.com.au)_
- Never leave your folding arm awning in the off/on position when you are not around, as it is sensitive to wind (gusts) and this can result in considerable damage.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Archiproducts lists BX270 max drop as 3.5 m (vs. Harol's own Lux page stating 4 m) and box dimensions as 162 x 227 mm (vs. Harol's 240 x 250 mm) — possible difference between BX270 standard and the 'Lux' variant.  _(källa: archiproducts.com)_
- Mounting area strength is a concern: awnings are heavy and under windy conditions can exercise quite a load; mounting on brick veneer walls may require securing to eave/rafters or using a structural beam.  _(källa: forums.whirlpool.net.au)_

## Vanliga problem

- Cracks appear in the facade to which the folding arm awning cassette is mounted and/or it comes loose — usually an indication that your facade is not resistant to the leverage caused by your folding arm awning.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Cassette no longer closes completely, leaving a small part of the sunshade visible (and sometimes discoloured) — usually because almost all sunshades expand over time; usually remedied by reprogramming the motor setting.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Front panel hangs higher on one side than the other — can usually be adjusted using the adjusting screws; requires holding up the affected jib arm during adjustment.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Fabric no longer neatly taut — usually because fabrics expand over time; typically fixable by reprogramming the motor adjustment.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Awning no longer responds to wireless remote control — usually means battery needs replacing; reprogramming may be necessary.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Facades that are not resistant to the leverage caused by the awning (e.g., weak brick veneer without proper reinforcement). May need chemical anchors, extended roof supports or an alternative product such as a freestanding pergola awning.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Windy backyards or locations exposed to frequent wind gusts — awning is sensitive to wind and cannot be left unattended when extended.  _(källa: forums.whirlpool.net.au)_

## Praktiska tips

- Check for obstructions such as protruding pipes or electrical wires before installation. Identify suitable mounting points that can support the weight of the awning and withstand external forces such as wind.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- Choose the appropriate bracket type based on the mounting surface and ensure a secure attachment to prevent any movement or instability. Properly positioning the brackets is crucial for overall stability and functionality.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- After installation, extend and retract the awning several times to check for smooth operation and proper alignment.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
- Every year use any reputable brand of light oil to lubricate moving parts. Check the awning from time to time to make sure all bolts and nuts are tightened.  _(källa: lifestyleawnings.com.au)_
- Dry the fabric in the extended position when wet as soon as possible.  _(källa: lifestyleawnings.com.au)_
- For cleaning frames: use mild detergent and warm water. Do not use abrasive materials, cleansers, chemicals, bleach or solvents. For fabric: wipe up minor spots with mild soap and a damp cloth; usually sufficient to brush off dirt.  _(källa: lifestyleawnings.com.au)_
- Use your folding arm awning regularly and watch out for any abnormalities and act on them immediately. After a period of non-use, always pay extra attention.  _(källa: luvifem.nl)_
- Awnings often need to be secured to the eave/rafters or use a beam on brick veneer houses to avoid wall collapse.  _(källa: forums.whirlpool.net.au)_
- If the awning is motorised, take precautions to ensure proper wiring and electrical connections to prevent the risk of electrical hazards.  _(källa: undercoverblinds.com.au)_
