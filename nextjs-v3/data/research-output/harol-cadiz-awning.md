> Cadiz protects any patio against excessive heat, year in, year out. This high-end sun canopy also offers various options including dimmable LED lighting in the box, arms and extension pole and an extra-large valance. Awning Cadiz stands out due to its closed, rounded-edge box. Thanks to this closed box, the fabric is protected against dirt and will look amazing for years. Even when the sun is low, you can protect your terrace and house from the heat, thanks to the dynamic vario-valance (up to 2 m) integrated into the extension pole. This sun canopy, which has a drop of up to 4 metres and has a maximum width of 7 metres, offers the largest area of shade on the market. Cadiz can be linked to provide even more shade!
