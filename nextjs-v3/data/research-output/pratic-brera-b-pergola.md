## Begränsningar och negativ info


## Begränsningar

- Brera B cannot be coupled into multiple modules depthwise/widthwise, unlike Brera S and P. It is designed for single-module installations within courtyards and existing structures.  _(källa: architonic.com)_
- Maximum dimensions for single module Brera B: 430L × 585P cm, with max height not specified separately (Brera S max H is 270 cm, Brera P is 300 cm).  _(källa: architonic.com)_
- Brera B is not compatible with Connect (fixed panels) or Glass Series panoramic perimeter glass walls, which are available only for Brera P and S.  _(källa: architonic.com)_
- Brera B is not compatible with Set (aluminium platform). Set is available only for Brera P and S.  _(källa: architonic.com)_
- Pratic does not offer dual mechanism (tilt + retract) for blades – only retractable. The double mechanism is described as 'probabilmente troppo problematico e poco affidabile' (probably too problematic and unreliable) according to forum discussion.  _(källa: arredamento.it)_

## Vanliga problem

- Water infiltration ('acqua che entra dappertutto') reported by multiple Trustpilot reviewers on Pratic bioclimatic pergolas.  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_
- LED lighting failures – LEDs not working, transformers fusing one after another on Brera installation (3-module 8x4m unit from 2020).  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_
- Glass panels not sliding properly reported as an issue.  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_
- After-sales support is dealer-dependent; Pratic states 'produce e commercializza i suoi prodotti attraverso dei rivenditori autonomi e non sempre siamo a conoscenza delle possibili problematiche insorte' (manufactures and sells through independent dealers and is not always aware of issues).  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_

## Olämpliga lägen

- Brera B is not suitable for very large spaces requiring multiple coupled modules – it is limited to single-module configurations unlike Brera S and P.  _(källa: architonic.com)_

## Återförsäljarkunskap


## Praktiska tips

- Forum dealer/installer recommends: 'Cerca un rivenditore installatore serio' (Find a serious dealer-installer) – implying dealer quality matters significantly for this product category.  _(källa: arredamento.it)_
- For registration at the Pratic customer care portal, the product serial number from the Usage and Warranty document (or label on the product) is required.  _(källa: it.trustpilot.com)_
- The Brera can be offered as an alternative to retractable awnings for customers considering awnings – but note that awnings (e.g. Pratic T-Hide) must be retracted in minimum wind and cannot take rain, so a pergola is a different category of protection.  _(källa: arredamento.it)_
