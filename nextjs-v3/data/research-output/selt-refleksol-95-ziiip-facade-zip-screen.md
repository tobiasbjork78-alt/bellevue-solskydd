## Praktiska tips

- When ordering both narrow and wide reflectors simultaneously, it is possible to use new WIDENED bottom beams also in reflectors over 1600 mm wide (for an additional fee of ~5%).  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- For widened bottom beam on Ziiip 95, the use of spacer brackets is recommended.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Depending on the dimensions of the roller blind, different bottom beams (narrow vs. wide) are used. The type is selected automatically based on the dimensions. When ordering multiple blinds of different sizes, the bottom beams may differ.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- When mounting directly to the wall, it is recommended to use at least one spacer (noted for the 120 system; analogous logic applies to the 95).  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
- Optionally, for an additional fee, a smooth cassette can be chosen instead of the standard bracket-mounted cassette — mainly applicable when mounting the screen on a pergola.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- The electric cable is approximately 2 metres long, exiting from a hole on the side or back of the cassette.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Vanliga felval

- If mounting is on a flat surface without brackets (e.g. wall, frame), the wide bottom beam will extend beyond the guide outline, making mounting impossible — the narrow beam must be used in this case. If unsure which beam will be applied, contact the seller.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_

## Monteringskunskap

- S = total width including brackets (up to 270 cm); H = total height (up to 300 cm). Measurement is for the complete system including strip guides.  _(källa: e-markizy.pl)_
- Guide mounting methods: direct mounting to the wall (front), direct mounting to the wall (side mounting), self-supporting version, self-supporting version with articulated handles, or butt mounting (wall version).  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
