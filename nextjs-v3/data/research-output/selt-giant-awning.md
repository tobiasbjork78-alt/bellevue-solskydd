- Generic retractable awning issue: Joints dry out and seize up without lubrication. Elbow joints and torque arms stiffen; movement becomes jerky, noisy, or stops altogether. - Generic maintenance: Lubricate retractable awning joints twice yearly (spring and fall) with dry silicone spray; target roller tube axle, elbow joints, brackets, and pitch crank  _(källa: marygrove.com)_
- Generic maintenance: Clean all pivot points with mild soap and water before lubricating to prevent trapping debris inside joints; let everything dry fully before spraying  _(källa: marygrove.com)_
- Generic maintenance: Avoid over-lubrication — use short bursts; excess lubricant attracts dust and accelerates wear instead of reducing it  _(källa: marygrove.com)_
- Generic advice: Never force a stuck awning to extend or retract — this often causes tears at stress points. Instead, investigate the underlying mechanical issue.  _(källa: cflawnings.com)_
