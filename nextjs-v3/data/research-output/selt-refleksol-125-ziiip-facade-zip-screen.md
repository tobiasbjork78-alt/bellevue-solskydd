## Praktiska tips

- The first step is to take precise measurements of the window opening or the installation location. It is important to take into account the dimensions of the box, guides and bottom strip to ensure an aesthetic and functional effect.  _(källa: door-filipek.pl)_
- In ZIP systems, it is crucial to precisely match the guides to the bottom strip to avoid play and uneven fabric guidance.  _(källa: door-filipek.pl)_
- The fabric is fed into the ZIP guides and hooked into the tensioning mechanism. It is important that the material tension is optimal, which will prevent the surface from creasing.  _(källa: door-filipek.pl)_
- Matching to unusual facade colors – powder coating of aluminium elements in any RAL colour allows for harmonious integration with the building's architecture.  _(källa: door-filipek.pl)_
- When ordering or pricing, we always provide the total external dimensions of the cover, including the cassette.  _(källa: shopsun.eu)_
